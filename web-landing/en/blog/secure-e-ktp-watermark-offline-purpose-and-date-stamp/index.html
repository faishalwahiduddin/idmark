Pick a photo and type the purpose#

The e-KTP watermark carries the purpose text and usage date, making your photo hard to misuse for other applications. Free-text purpose fits whatever the requesting party asks for.

Automatic date stamp#

The date stamp fills automatically when the watermark is created. The date records when the photo was stamped so the purpose period is documented.

Adjust position, size, and opacity#

Place the watermark to cover key photo areas without killing data legibility. Size and opacity slide until the result balances protection and readability.
