Protect KTP Copies: Safe Habits Before Sharing

By Faishal · 2026-10-04 · 4 min read

Safe KTP copies start with habits, not just an app. Always stamp purpose and date, verify the recipient, and archive every share. IDMark prepares offline watermarks so the good habit is easy to keep.

Stamp before sending

Make stamping a mandatory step before identity photos leave the gallery. The habit closes the most common gap: plain photos with no usage boundary circulating freely.

Pre-share checklist

Checklist: purpose written, date filled, preview checked. Three steps taking under a minute per document. [STAT-NEEDED: plain identity photos circulating boundlessly] [CITE-NEEDED: identity-photocopy misuse reports]

Know your document recipients

Share documents only with parties you trust who genuinely need them. Ask for clarity when a recipient does not explain why the photo is requested.

Log every share: when, to whom, for what need. This simple archive backs you up if a photo is ever misused.
