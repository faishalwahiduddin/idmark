Afraid Your Shared ID Photo Will Be Misused?

ID cards, passports, and licenses shared without a watermark can be reused for applications you never approved.

Once an identity photo spreads, you cannot take it back. Without a purpose-and-date stamp, the digital copy can serve fake verifications, while online editors upload your sensitive documents to their servers.

IDMark is the answer. An independent utility app (not a government app, affiliated with no agency): stamp free-text purpose and automatic date onto ID, passport, license, and family-card photos, adjust position, size, and opacity, preview results, and keep a stamp history. 100% on-device, no account, no server.
